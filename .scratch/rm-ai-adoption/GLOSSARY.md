# RM AI adoption strategy

The language for the infographics that present the plan for adopting AI in research management. It is separate from the Agentic OS language in the root glossary.

## Language

**Strategy**:
The RM AI adoption strategy: the plan for using AI to solve research-management problems, under which the three mechanisms are presented as one.
_Avoid_: Framework (that is the Agentic OS tool framework), roadmap (that is the AI 2027 roadmap)

**Mechanism**:
One of the three projects the strategy rests on, seen as a means of automating, simplifying and speeding up research-management work.
_Avoid_: Project, tool (that is a catalogued entry of the framework), pillar, product

**Twofold**:
The mechanism that shows the calls open now and the funded track record in one place, to inform the decision to commit proposal effort.
_Avoid_: Knowledge graph, knowledge-graph-app, EU Graphs

**Proposal Orchestrator**:
The mechanism that turns call, rule and project sources into a proposal through a chain of phases, each closed by a gate.
_Avoid_: Proposal writer, proposal_orchestrator, the pipeline

**Budget Engine**:
The mechanism that builds a proposal's budget workbook and computes its figures by fixed code.
_Avoid_: Excel module, excel_module, budget tool

**Path**:
The three steps of a proposal's life that the overview is drawn along: choose the call, write the proposal, build the budget. Each step has one mechanism.
_Avoid_: Pipeline, lifecycle, workflow, journey

**Rule**:
One of the four ways every mechanism behaves: a person decides, figures come from fixed code, every claim cites its source, and a declared failure beats a made-up result.
_Avoid_: Principle, guardrail, policy, safeguard

**Infographic**:
One fixed 16:9 picture of the strategy or of a single mechanism, which can be exported and used on its own.
_Avoid_: Slide, poster, panel, page

**Overview**:
The infographic that shows the whole strategy and how the three mechanisms fit together.
_Avoid_: Summary, cover, landing

**Stage**:
How far a mechanism has come: built, in use, or scaling next. Every mechanism shows exactly one as current.
_Avoid_: Status (that is a tool's status in the Agentic OS), phase (that is a roadmap phase), maturity

**Benefit**:
One of the five things a mechanism does for research-management work: Automate, Simplify, Accelerate, Innovate or Adapt. A mechanism delivers one or more.
_Avoid_: Value, outcome, capability, feature

**Ask**:
One thing the department is requested to grant so the strategy can go ahead. The overview holds all of them together.
_Avoid_: Requirement, request, need (that is a capability need of the AI 2027 roadmap)

**Lead**:
Who drives a mechanism: its author, or colleagues with the author's support.
_Avoid_: Owner, maintainer, role (that is a working position in the Agentic OS partnership)

**Hosting statement**:
The plain account on a mechanism's infographic of where its data sits, which model processes it and where a person signs off.
_Avoid_: Security claim, compliance badge, guarantee

**Next step**:
The single thing a mechanism needs or does next, shown as one callout on its infographic.
_Avoid_: Milestone, action item, ask
