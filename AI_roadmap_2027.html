Sequenced 2027 Roadmap

The roadmap has five phases from now to December 2027. The order follows two rules in the document: secure the downside before investing (S5's short-, mid- and long-term risk order) and build foundations before applications (S8's curriculum order). The timing anchors are S2's predicted H1 2027 demand surge and the expected December–March benchmark saturation. The phase dates themselves are [Inference].

P0 Nov–Dec 2026

Phase 0 — Stabilize and baseline

Topics
security prep, worry window, TLC self-score, personal eval, one AI task a day.
Milestones
resilience kit and MFA in place (S5). A 10–15-task personal eval exists (S5). Your role is scored on Trust, Learn and Contact (S4).
Rationale
you can't track change without a baseline. S5 tackles short-term security risk first because it is cheap ("15 minutes"). The worry window cuts the noise that blocks action (S4).
P1 Jan–Feb 2027

Phase 1 — Foundations and tooling

Topics
the one-week curriculum (S8); one chat assistant plus one harness; climbing the trust ladder by giving agents boring, checkable work first (S6); moving off drag-and-drop builders (S2).
Milestones
curriculum done. First reusable skills or routines built. Personal eval re-run on each new model.
Rationale
S8: "I wish I had done all of this ahead of time before I learned the tools". Benchmark saturation expected between December and March makes tool fluency urgent (S2).
P2 Feb–Apr 2027

Phase 2 — Judgment layer

Topics
writing specs and rubrics, unknown-unknowns interviews ("grill me"), a shared domain vocabulary, reviewing only high-blast-radius code, eval design (S1, S6, S3).
Milestones
a written definition of "good" for one domain or client type. An eval suite that runs each skill about 10 times with scoring (S3).
Rationale
S1 bets this is where the bottleneck "stays" for 10 years. Every later phase depends on it: consulting delivery (S3 step 5), product engineering (S1) and job-proofing (S5 evals).
P3 Mar–Jun 2027

Phase 3 — Go to market in the demand window

Topics
RACE diagnosis → goal factoring → build → deploy → evals → team sync (S3); a packaged workshop and team-upskilling offer (S2); a distribution and sales system, 100x over technical work (S2, S5).
Milestones
measurement baseline agreed with the first client. First result delivered against a guarantee. Workshop offer live. For employed engineers [Inference]: a product- or forward-deployed-engineer role or project secured.
Rationale
S2 predicts most business owners wake up in H1 2027. Selling without Phases 1–2 means "automating broken processes" (S3).
P4 Jul–Dec 2027

Phase 4 — Shift up, consolidate, hedge

Topics
moving from implementation to education and embedded advisory (S2); handing over IP through skills and routines (S3); ownership and relationships (S5); downshifting where the job no longer fits (S7); short-lived, asymmetric opportunities (S2).
Milestones
recurring retainer or IP-based revenue. One ownership asset or channel. Re-scored TLC and eval. A 2028 position decided.
Rationale
S2 warns 2027 may be "the beginning of its decline" and admits "I don't know" beyond it. S1 expects operations and security to take 5–10 years to automate, so judgment-heavy work keeps its value longest.

  3. 3
    Requirements-interview tooling: an agent that questions the user relentlessly to surface unknown unknowns before building P2S6
  4. 4

  5. 5

  6. 6

  7. 7
